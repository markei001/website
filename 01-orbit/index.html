The journey

From your sofa
to zero-g.

No pilot's licence. No PhD. Just four steps between you and the most beautiful view in the solar system.

01 — TRAIN

Two weeks in the Atacama.

Centrifuge sessions, zero-g parabolas and a lot of stargazing at our base in the driest desert on Earth.

02 — LAUNCH

Eight minutes of thunder.

Our reusable Halcyon rocket takes you from 0 to 28,000 km/h. Your coffee will float before the music ends.

03 — DRIFT

Weightless, finally.

Unbuckle. Somersault. Watch sixteen sunrises a day through a window the size of a living room.

04 — ARRIVE

Step onto another world.

Leave a footprint that will outlast the pyramids. Then come home and try to explain it to everyone.
