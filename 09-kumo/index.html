one for each season.

旅 THE JOURNEY

Seven days,
no rush.

Our signature route from Tokyo to Kyoto, by bullet train, mountain railway and on foot. Luggage travels ahead of you.

一
DAY 1 · TOKYO

Lanterns of Asakusa

Arrive, breathe, and wander the old Shitamachi streets as the temple lights flicker on.

二
DAY 2 · HAKONE

Fuji from the water

Pirate ship across Lake Ashi, then a ryokan with a private hot spring facing the mountain.

三
DAY 3 · KISO VALLEY

The old post road

Walk the Nakasendō between Magome and Tsumago — eight kilometres of cedar forest and waterfalls.

四
DAY 4 · TAKAYAMA

Morning markets

Miso, sake breweries and Hida beef skewers in a perfectly preserved Edo-era town.

五
DAY 5 · KYOTO

Ten thousand gates

Climb Fushimi Inari at sunrise, when it's just you, the foxes and the vermilion torii.

六
DAY 6 · ARASHIYAMA

The bamboo sea

Zazen with a monk, a shōjin-ryōri temple lunch, and a slow boat down the Hozu river.

七
DAY 7 · NARA

Bowing deer

Say goodbye under the Great Buddha. The deer will bow to you. Bow back.
