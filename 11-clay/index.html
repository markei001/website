Method

The curve is the machine's. The wobble is Marta's.

1

A seed

Four digits decide the form, the lean of the belly, how deep the throwing rings run, and which two glazes will meet. Same number, same pot. Always.

2

A rib

Seven control points make a spline. We cut it in 3 mm birch at full size and Marta throws against it, one hand inside, the rib outside. Every pot ships with its own rib in the box.

3

A firing

Bisque to 1000 °C, dipped by hand, glaze fired to 1222 °C in oxidation. The foot is waxed bare so the clay shows. Cobalt moves in the heat; no two dips break the same way.

Cobalt Slip. 2.1% cobalt carbonate over white slip. Runs. Pools in the bottom of cups.

Glazes

Seven calm glazes. One loud one.

Everything is earth, ash and soot, except the cobalt. We don't tone it down. It shows up on roughly one piece in four, usually as a dip.
