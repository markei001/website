How the room is built

There are no audio files. A tiny score walks between six chords every twelve to eighteen seconds, keeping the notes two chords share so the voices lead instead of jump. Pads are paired detuned sawtooths under a slowly breathing low-pass; bells are a sine plus an inharmonic partial. All of it runs through a delay and a four-second convolution reverb we generated from decaying noise.

Silence is a signal too

With sound off, the rooms listen to a simulation that runs the same score: the same notes, the same harmonics, the same bell echoes, computed as a spectrum instead of heard. The microphone, if you allow it, is analysed in the browser and is never recorded or played back; the synth steps aside while it listens.
