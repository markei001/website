§ 01 — StatementRead slowly. The ink arrives as you scroll.
01

Statement

Every compound in this archive is fictional. None was synthesised, weighed or tested. They are filed with the care owed to real ones: a number, a name, a formula, a mass, a shelf. The subject is the grid. Nothing here is medical information, and nothing here is for use.
