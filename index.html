Fifteen sites,
fifteen ways to wow.

Each site shows a different way to do scroll animation and 3D. Click one, scroll slowly, and notice what makes you go “ooh”. Then make it your own. Every site is a single index.html you can open and read.

How to top the contest

1. One big idea

Every site here has one “hero moment”: flying through a galaxy, walking a museum, diving the ocean. Pick yours first, then build everything around it.

2. Scroll = story

Use scroll to tell a story with a start, middle and end: dry planet to green, surface to sea floor, sunrise to night. Judges remember a story better than a list of effects.

3. Two fonts, three colours

Pick one display font and one body font from Google Fonts, plus a tiny palette. It's the cheapest way to look professional.

4. Make it touchable

Add one thing to play with: a colour picker, a drag-to-spin can, a “plant a tree” button. People who can interact with it remember it.

5. Check it on a phone

Open it on a phone before you present. Shrink the 3D, stack the columns, and make sure no text is hidden behind the art.

6. Know your code

Judges might ask “how does this work?” Read the comments in these files. Being able to explain IntersectionObserver or a camera path is a big plus.

All brands are made up. Built with plain HTML/CSS/JS plus Three.js and GSAP from a CDN, so you need internet for the 3D and fonts to load.
